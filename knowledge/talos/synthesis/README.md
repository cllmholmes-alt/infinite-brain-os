# Synthesis: talos

This folder holds derived intellectual work that is neither raw archive nor settled canon.

## What goes here

- The TALOS-to-brain relationship map: the central open question of whether TALOS is the
  runtime substrate this brain governs, a peer system, or a successor.
- Governance-model comparison: where TALOS doctrine and the infinite-brain-os control model
  agree, differ, and could unify.
- Canon-candidates proposed for promotion into `canon/`.

## What does not go here

Provenance and ingestion receipts go in `support/`. Settled doctrine goes in `canon/` or
`pillars/`.

## Promotion path

A synthesis node that reaches sufficient confidence becomes a canon-candidate. The operator
reviews it and, if approved, it is compressed into `core-doctrine.md` with a changelog entry.
