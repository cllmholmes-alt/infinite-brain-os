# Cognitive load is structural, not moral

## The claim

Cognitive load is a structural issue, not a moral failing. Executive-function difficulty is
something the environment and the tools should fix, not something the user should be blamed
for. This is the single load-bearing claim of ADHD-OS.

## Why it matters here

This claim is the test every feature is held against. A feature that reduces structural load
aligns with the claim. A feature that adds load, captures attention, or moralises struggle
violates it. There is no neutral ground: a feature either moves toward less structural load
or away from it.

## What follows from it

- The product is calm by design. Stimulation and engagement-maximising patterns are
  forbidden, not merely avoided.
- The product is capacity-aware. It mediates execution by the user's current capacity rather
  than assuming an unlimited user.
- The product's language is structural, not moralising. Copy describes the structure being
  fixed, never the user failing.
